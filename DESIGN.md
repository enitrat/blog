# Design System: Mathieu Saugier

## Overview

**Creative North Star: “The Living Editorial Index.”**

One editorial world spans the homepage, indexes, reading sheets, About, work reports, 404, header, and footer. It is light paper and near-black ink, structured by cobalt rules, with orange reserved for active moments.

The homepage is a Living Index: explicit identity, separate Work, Projects, and Writing groups, one panoramic art stage, then a quiet contact close. Other routes use the ruled-sheet pattern. Reading pages add a measured prose column and optional margin apparatus; the article remains complete without it.

**Key characteristics:**

- Cool paper, near-black ink, cobalt structure, rare orange events.
- Literata for identity and editorial hierarchy; Commissioner for reading and metadata; Roboto Mono for code only.
- Square corners, fine rules, measured prose, no cards or app chrome.
- Shared header, footer, masthead, ledger row, article body, and contact primitives.
- Placeholder copy, role details, contact destinations, and generated artwork remain visibly provisional.

## Colors

Paper and ink carry the resting state. Cobalt explains structure; orange marks a state or event.

### Primary

- **Cobalt Structure:** rules, links, dates, labels, active titles, markers, and code borders.
- **Cobalt Deep:** selected code syntax where the structure color needs more contrast.

### Secondary

- **Orange Event:** navigation underline, one active index or contents mark, copy confirmation, and selected artwork moments. It is not the focus color; focus uses cobalt for contrast.

### Neutral

- **Cool Paper:** the page ground with an almost imperceptible fiber texture.
- **Near-Black Ink:** primary text, structural rules, and dark image/code fields.
- **Muted Ink:** supporting text, captions, footer text, and inactive apparatus.
- **Neutral Dark:** code strings.
- **Paper White:** text and rules over dark artwork.
- **Cobalt Rule, Wash, Underline:** restrained dividers, inline-code ground, and prose-link underline.

**Orange Event Rule:** use orange sparingly; one active mark per surface at most.

**Cobalt Structure Rule:** prefer a fine rule or label over a filled blue container.

**Own-Colours Code Rule:** code uses the site's palette on a transparent ground, never an imported terminal theme.

## Typography

**Display:** Literata Variable, with Georgia fallback.

**Body:** Commissioner Variable, with Arial fallback.

**Code:** Roboto Mono, with the platform monospace stack.

Literata carries identity and editorial hierarchy. Commissioner carries prose, navigation, labels, metadata, and apparatus. The type system has no fourth voice.

### Hierarchy

- **Headline:** fluid Literata, up to `6rem`, for mastheads and major homepage titles.
- **Title:** Literata at descending sizes for index groups, entries, books, stage captions, and onward links.
- **Prose headings:** Literata `h2`–`h4`; `h2` receives a cobalt rule.
- **Body:** Commissioner at `1rem`/`1.55`; article prose is fluid at `1.02rem`–`1.15rem` with `1.68` leading.
- **Labels and metadata:** Commissioner below `1rem`; below `0.7rem` is reserved for labels, dates, and markers.

**True Measure Rule:** article prose stays at `52ch`, which produces a readable line length in this typeface. Do not widen it to compensate for unused space.

## Layout

All routes use one centered shell, the shared header/footer, and the page gutter tokens. Non-home routes open with `page-head`: headline, optional standfirst, then a near-black rule.

The homepage places orientation beside separate Work/Projects and Writing indexes. The art stage spans the content width; the contact close follows it without repeating the indexes.

Reading pages use a centered `52ch` spine. The margin is optional apparatus, bounded and independently scrollable. Code and tables may use a wider track; ordinary reading matter, images, and figures stay with the spine. Indexes and the bookshelf use ruled groups and shared ledger/shelf rows.

At `980px`, homepage orientation becomes a full-width band while the two index channels remain paired. At `760px`, the shell becomes one column, navigation stacks, indexes stack, and the art stage becomes a sticky `min(42svh, 24rem)` band with an `18rem` floor.

**Segmented Index Rule:** Work, Projects, and Writing keep separate headings, lists, and destinations.

**One-Panorama Rule:** the homepage opening has one dominant art stage. Non-home reading and index surfaces carry no artwork.

**Apparatus-Not-Navigation Rule:** margin contents are enhancement; prose and links work without JavaScript.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from paper texture, scale, spacing, rules, crop, and the dark art stage. No surface shadows, cards, glass, blur, or floating panels. A localized caption text shadow is allowed for legibility over variable artwork.

Motion is restrained: artwork crossfades, active rows shift color and position without reflow, and navigation underlines wipe. Reduced motion leaves a stable still composition and disables smooth scrolling.

## Shapes

All surfaces, media, code blocks, and controls are square-cornered. One-pixel rules distinguish structural boundaries, internal segmentation, and deliberate emphasis. Arrows are authored SVG line work; ratings are tabular numerals; there is no glyph icon system.

The eight-point orange star is the only ornamental mark and indicates active state. It must not become a decorative motif or multiply across a surface.

## Components

Atomic Design is the implementation rule: shared atoms and primitives own repeated visual structure; molecules compose them; surfaces assemble the shared shell, masthead, article body, ledger, shelf, contact, and art-stage patterns. A named modifier is valid only when a surface genuinely varies. Page styles must not fork a shared primitive's layout rules.

### Site header and footer

- Header: serif identity, four route links, one near-black rule; mobile keeps all four destinations visible.
- Footer: identity, route links, RSS, contact destinations, and year in muted ink. It stays flat and unruled.
- Links use a cobalt focus outline and an orange underline on hover, focus, and current state.

### Page head

- Literata headline, optional muted standfirst, and a near-black rule.
- Work, Writing, and Bookshelf mastheads have no standfirst; reading surfaces use one only when it adds authored orientation.

### Writing ledger and shelf rows

- Ruled rows replace cards. The whole row is one link; state changes color and uses transform, never reflow.
- Writing rows use date, title, description, and decorative arrow. Translations are reached from the article, not listed separately.
- Shelf rows use status, year, title, author, and numeric rating. Covers and star glyphs are not used.

### Marginalia sheet and article body

- The margin carries facts, translations, and one `h2`/`h3` contents list. Its active mark follows the reading position without changing geometry.
- Article prose uses the `52ch` measure, cobalt links and markers, ruled `h2` headings, readable blockquotes, constrained figures, scrollable tables, and site-colour code blocks.
- The close offers related or adjacent pieces plus clear exits; no page dead-ends.

### Homepage index and art stage

- Work, Projects, and Writing each have an explicit heading, destination, ruled entries, and authored arrow.
- Hover/focus selects artwork on wide screens; narrow screens select the entry nearest the reading line. Links remain ordinary links.
- The stage has one visible, accessible frame at a time. Artwork supports browsing and carries no essential copy or navigation.

### About and work reports

- About uses the shared masthead, authored prose, and a facts column; it has no photo, CV, or career timeline.
- Work reports use the Marginalia Sheet and describe situation, role, decisions, outcome, and lessons when authored. External project links are secondary actions.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** use the shared shell and primitives on every route.
- **Do** preserve paper, ink, cobalt structure, rare orange events, square geometry, and the `52ch` reading measure.
- **Do** keep Work, Projects, and Writing distinct.
- **Do** make enhancement optional and preserve a meaningful no-script state.
- **Do** keep placeholder claims and generated artwork visibly provisional.
- **Don't** add a second visual world, generic card grid, rounded controls, gradients, glass, app chrome, decorative grid, drop shadow, or glyph icon set.
- **Don't** use artwork as hidden navigation or decorative filler.
- **Don't** widen the article measure, let apparatus become the only navigation, or list translations as archive entries.
- **Don't** use orange for routine text, focus, ordinary code literals, or broad surfaces.
- **Don't** invent professional claims, availability language, contact destinations, or funnel-style calls to action.
