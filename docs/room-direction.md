# Living-room direction

The room should read as a dark, inhabited library at first glance, while the
books and writing remain usable. The two September 22 references set the mood:
dark green walls, brown oak joinery, oxblood or worn-leather seating, a dense
Persian rug, warm practical lamps, cool window fill, and small pools of light.

Keep the current isometric cutaway and the working bookshelf, record player,
and writing desk. Do not chase offline-render detail that disappears at the
room's web size or breaks the 11 MB initial asset budget.

## Next passes

The remaining gap is material richness, not composition. If another pass is
warranted, spend it on broad, bake-visible surface variation in leather, wood,
and the rug. Do not add small props or increase texture size unless a camera
comparison shows a clear gain.

Each pass should change one visual family and use the smallest valid bake. Keep
it only after a camera render and `room:verify` show an improvement at desktop
and laptop sizes.
