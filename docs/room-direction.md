# Living room direction

The room should read at first glance as a dark library someone lives in, with
working books, record player, and writing desk.

## The look

Two reference images, chosen on September 22, set the mood:

- dark green walls and brown oak joinery
- oxblood or worn-leather seating
- a dense Persian rug
- warm lamps that make small pools of light
- cool light from the window

Keep the isometric cutaway and all three interactive objects. Skip detail
that vanishes at the room's page size or would push the GLBs past 11 MB.

## Where the room stands

The September 22 pass matched the references' composition and mood:

- oxblood seating, a leather desk chair, and a round marble-topped table
- darker oak, parquet, green walls, and a near-black surround
- shelf greenery, and burgundy curtains on a brass rod
- smaller light pools, deeper corners, and a closer 4:3 view

## What to do next

The composition is right. Material richness is missing. Add broad surface
variation to the leather, wood, and rug, the kind that survives baking. Add
small props or larger textures only if a camera render shows a clear gain.

Change one material family per pass, and bake only its groups. Keep the pass
if `bun run room:check chromium --shots` shows a gain at desktop and laptop
sizes.
