# About Me

Hi, I'm Mathieu, a Software Engineer. I currently work as a Senior Protocol Engineer at [Zama](/work/zama/), a company building Fully Homomorphic Encryption for blockchain and AI. Before that, I was the CTO of [Kakarot](/work/kakarot/), where we developed efficient Zero-Knowledge Proof systems for client-side proving.

I like building products end to end, and I keep building outside of work: I wrote a book about the [Cairo Programming Language](https://book.cairo-lang.org), an AI conversational agent for the [Starknet Ecosystem](/work/starknet-agent/), and an [API for Cairo code generation](/work/cairo-coder/) that can be plugged into any agentic tool using MCP.

Before any of this, I funded part of my studies by finding an edge in market-making strategies. I have also written a few angel checks for friends building companies, and am generally interested in investments and economics.

I have a Master's degree in Engineering from [INSA Lyon](https://www.insa-lyon.fr/), and a Master's degree in Computer Science from [Universität Passau](https://www.uni-passau.de/).
