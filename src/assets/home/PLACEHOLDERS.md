All final assets were converted with `cwebp`. The generated PNG originals and the lossless `paper-fiber.seamless.png` have been **deleted** — together they were ~18MB that nothing imports, for artwork that is meant to be replaced. Everything needed to regenerate them is still here: the full prompt for each image lives in its `.webp.json` sidecar and in `prompts/`. Regenerate at the documented dimensions, then convert with `cwebp` before committing.
