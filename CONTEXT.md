# Personal Site

This context defines the language used to organize Mathieu's personal space. Its vocabulary keeps the product centered on authored work and relationships rather than drifting toward a résumé, portfolio template, or activity feed.

## Language

**Personal Space**:
The whole site as an evergreen expression of Mathieu's work, thinking, interests, and visual world. It creates recognition and conversation without advertising his availability.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, personal brand, candidate site

**Guided Path**:
The concise narrative sequence that orients a first-time visitor before offering deeper exploration. It presents identity, thesis, proof, personal world, and an invitation to connect.
_Avoid_: Funnel, landing-page flow

**Portal**:
A clear entry from the Guided Path into a deeper content area such as Work, Writing, Bookshelf, or About. A Portal is explicit navigation, not a hidden hotspot or puzzle.
_Avoid_: Feature card, secret interaction

**Work**:
The authored record of significant systems and products Mathieu helped shape. It emphasizes responsibilities, decisions, outcomes, and lessons rather than employment chronology.
_Avoid_: Résumé, career timeline, project gallery

**Field Report**:
A Work piece that explains a real project's situation, Mathieu's role, a consequential architectural or product decision, its outcome, and what he learned.
_Avoid_: Case study, project card, achievement blurb

**Writing**:
The single collection for Mathieu's authored essays, technical guides, project reflections, and book reflections, regardless of length.
_Avoid_: Blog, Notes and Essays

**Bookshelf**:
Mathieu's reading record, combining a complete personal archive with a curated layer that helps visitors understand his interests. It holds reading metadata; substantial responses to books are Writing.
_Avoid_: Reads, content feed

**Book Reflection**:
A Writing piece in which Mathieu develops an idea or response prompted by a book. It is more than a rating, summary, or reading-status update.
_Avoid_: Book log, review card

**Artwork**:
An image selected to shape atmosphere, connect subjects, or support a specific story or idea throughout the Personal Space. Artwork is connective material rather than a separate content destination.
_Avoid_: AI decoration, gallery item, hero wallpaper

**About**:
A concise orientation to Mathieu as a person and practitioner, connecting his professional center with the interests visible elsewhere in the site.
_Avoid_: Bio résumé, personal pitch
